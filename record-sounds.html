Record letter sounds

A one-time job for whoever builds the app. Record each sound, save the file, and upload it to the repo as clips.json, replacing the empty one. Every device then plays these sounds. No parent or teacher needs this page.

Say the pure sound, not the letter name: "mmm" for m, not "em". Short sounds like /t/ and /p/ are quick puffs with no "uh" after them. A quiet room helps.
